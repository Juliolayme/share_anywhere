Share Anywhere — Brand identity

Direction: “Universal Transfer”. Two lanes moving in opposite directions — blue sends, green receives. Built from two capsules and two points so it survives at 16px and reads instantly as an exchange between any two endpoints.

Logo exploration

1aOrbit

Continuous loop = always reachable. Calm and round, but close to generic “sync/refresh” glyphs — weak ownership.

1bBridge

Two endpoints joined by a channel. Clear metaphor, but static — reads as “linked”, not “moving”. Channel disappears at 16px.

1c · SelectedPassing lanes

Two capsules pass each other; the dot marks each lane’s leading edge. Encodes direction (send →, ← receive), two-way exchange and any-to-any. Owns the blue/green split; holds at 16px.
