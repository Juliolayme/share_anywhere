Share Anywhere for iPhone

Same flows as Android, built with iOS patterns: tab bar, large titles, sheets, inset grouped lists, system alerts. Sharing works as a guest; only Clipboard needs sign-in. iOS pauses the app when it leaves the screen, so the keep-open reminder matters more here.
